The Problem

Revenue That Doesn't Pay the Bills

Northgate Logistics had grown top-line revenue 22% over two years — but operating margin had barely moved. The CFO suspected a handful of high-volume, low-margin contracts were consuming capacity that could serve more profitable customers.

At the same time, inventory planning was reactive: the operations team was routinely over-ordering in Q3 to buffer against Q4 demand spikes, tying up $1.2M in working capital unnecessarily. The ask was a margin breakdown by segment and a forward-looking model that could inform smarter inventory and contract decisions.

Methodology

How It Was Built

1
Margin Breakdown (SQL + Pandas) Calculated gross margin %, profit per unit, and YoY growth at Segment × Country granularity using SQL window functions. Pivoted results in Pandas and ranked top/bottom 5 combinations by margin efficiency — not revenue.
2
Time-Series Forecasting (Prophet) Trained Facebook Prophet on 7 quarters of quarterly gross profit data. Tuned seasonality settings to match Northgate's Q4 freight spike. Evaluated against ARIMA baseline — Prophet outperformed on MAPE (9.1% vs. 13.4%).
3
Scenario Modeling (Python) Built a parameterized what-if function in Python that adjusts the COGS assumption and recalculates gross profit, margin %, and variance-to-target for any input scenario. The CFO can now run scenarios without touching the model.
4
CFO Briefing Delivered Three-page written brief: current margin state, 2-quarter forecast under three scenarios, and a contract renegotiation recommendation for the two lowest-margin Channel accounts. Presented to the CFO and COO jointly.
