The Problem

Losing Customers Silently

Vantage Digital's customer success team was fighting churn reactively — reaching out only after users had already cancelled. With ~5,600 active subscribers and a monthly churn rate of 16.8%, the math was brutal: they were replacing nearly 1 in 5 customers every month just to stay flat.

The ask: build a model that flags at-risk customers 30 days before they cancel, and identify the behavioral signals that actually matter — so the CS team can intervene with the right message, not a generic email blast.

Methodology

How It Was Built

1
Cohort Analysis (Pandas) Grouped customers by signup month and calculated month-over-month retention rates. Identified that 60% of all churn occurs within the first 90 days — making early onboarding the highest-leverage intervention point.
2
Feature Engineering Created derived features: days_since_login, ticket_rate_30d, tenure_bucket, and payment_method_risk_score. Handled class imbalance (83/17 split) with SMOTE oversampling before model training.
3
Logistic Regression Model (Scikit-learn) Trained and cross-validated a logistic regression classifier. Final model: 83% accuracy, 79% precision on churn class. Extracted and ranked feature coefficients to identify the 5 most predictive variables.
4
Segmentation & Playbook Translated model scores into 4 actionable risk tiers with specific CS interventions for each. Delivered as a Google Slides deck for the customer success team with no assumed data knowledge.
