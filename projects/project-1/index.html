The Problem

Revenue ≠ Profit

Meridian Office Co. was reporting strong top-line sales growth, but overall margins had quietly compressed by 4 points over 18 months. The VP of Sales had a hunch: aggressive discounting in certain sub-categories was eating into profit faster than volume could compensate.

The challenge was that existing reporting only showed revenue by region — it couldn't isolate where discounts were being applied, or which product lines were structurally unprofitable. Leadership needed a single view that told the full story.

Key Findings

Where the Money Was Going

Tables (Furniture) lost money in every region Despite $200K+ in revenue, Tables posted a –$18K net loss across all regions — discount rates averaged 31%, wiping out all gross margin.
Office Supplies drove 68% of total profit on 41% of revenue Paper, binders, and storage products had minimal discounting and high repeat purchase rates — the true engine of the business.
The Central region over-discounted by 11 percentage points Same products, same cost structure — but Central's reps were offering an average 26% discount vs. 15% in the West, for no measurable lift in volume.

Methodology

How It Was Built

1
Data Cleaning (SQL + Pandas) Standardized date formats, removed 142 duplicate order IDs, corrected negative profit records caused by return-without-refund entries, and unified product category naming across 3 source files.
2
Exploratory Analysis (Python) Calculated profit margin at Order → Sub-Category → Region granularity. Ran correlation analysis between discount rate and profit margin (r = –0.71) to quantify the discount problem.
3
Dashboard Build (Tableau) Four-view dashboard: regional profit map, category waterfall chart, discount vs. profit scatter plot, and a YoY trend line with forecast. Designed for weekly review by the VP of Sales.
4
Recommendations Delivered One-page executive brief: cap furniture discounts at 15%, flag Central region reps for discount coaching, and shift Q4 promotional spend to high-margin Office Supplies.
